Confirmation of Jewishness

Accoding to Halacha(Jewish law)religious shidduch is possible between Jews, and the next step afte shidduch is Huppah. In order to comply with this law and be able to perform the huppah retual there is a procedure for confirming Jewishness.

We at Onesoul provide advice and assistance in collecting documents to confirm Jewishness - confirm@shiduh.org.
